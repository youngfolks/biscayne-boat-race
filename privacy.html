Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

The short version

Biscayne Bay Boat Race does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. From version 1.1 the game sends anonymous crash and problem reports so we can fix bugs; they contain nothing that identifies you.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things on your iPhone so it works the way you left it: your best race times, your sound settings, the course you last picked, and whether you have bought the Full Race Season. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Crash and problem reports

From version 1.1, if the game crashes, freezes, or a race against a friend runs into trouble (for example, a match that fails to start), it sends a report to Sentry, the service we use to find and fix bugs. A report contains what went wrong, the game version, your device model and iOS version, the steps of the race leading up to the problem, and a random number created when the game is installed, which lets us count how many players a problem affects and resets if you reinstall.

Reports never include your name, email address, Apple Account, Game Center nickname, location or IP address (Sentry is set not to store IP addresses). They are not linked to you, and are never used for advertising or tracking.

Racing a friend

Two-player races use Apple's Game Center. Apple handles sign-in, invitations and matchmaking. During a race, your iPhone sends your boat's position and your Game Center nickname to the other player's iPhone, through Apple. We don't store any of it. Game Center is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The Full Race Season is an in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account, or payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Crash and problem reports contain nothing that identifies a player.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
